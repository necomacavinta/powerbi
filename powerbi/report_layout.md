# Recommended Power BI report layout

## Page 1 — Executive Summary

KPI cards:
- Actual Revenue YTD
- Forecast Revenue Next 3 Months
- Forecast Revenue Next 6 Months
- Open Pipeline Value
- Closed Revenue
- Product Revenue
- Product Cost
- Forecast Gross Margin

Visuals:
- Line chart: Actual Revenue vs Forecast Revenue by Month
- Bar chart: Budget Hours vs Actual Hours by Month
- Matrix: Project Status by Project Segment
- KPI cards: project count, open pipeline value, signed revenue, closed revenue

## Page 2 — Trend and Forecast

Visuals:
- Line chart: Actual Revenue, Forecast Revenue, Budget Estimates by Month
- Area chart: Cumulative Actual Revenue vs Forecast Revenue
- Scatter chart: Project size vs forecast margin
- Slicer: Date, Project Status, Project Segment, Manager, Company

## Page 3 — Project Portfolio

Visuals:
- Table with project-level details:
  - Project_Name
  - Signed_Date
  - Date_Start
  - Closed_Date
  - Budget_Hours
  - Actual_Hours
  - Remaining_Hours
  - Project_Status
  - Project_Segment
  - Actual_Labor_Revenue
  - Forecast_Labor_Revenue
  - Product_Revenue
  - Product_Cost
  - Forecast_Gross_Margin

## Page 4 — Staffing and Capacity

Visuals:
- Team forecast by month
- Actual hours by team member
- Remaining hours by manager
- Forecast staffing need for next quarter

## Suggested slicers

- Date
- Project Status
- Project Segment
- Billing Model
- Project Type
- Manager
- Company
- Agreement

## Business use case

This dashboard answers:
- What revenue is already recognized?
- What is expected to come in over the next 1, 3, 6, and 12 months?
- How much of the pipeline is Time & Material vs Full Complete?
- What is the product revenue and cost burden on the portfolio?
- Which projects are likely to require additional team capacity?
